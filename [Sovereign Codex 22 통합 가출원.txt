Cognitive-Behavioral Integrity Authentication and Distributed Asset Settlement Infrastructure with 22 Core Functional Modules (The Sovereign Codex 22 System)

2. FIELD OF THE INVENTION
The present invention relates to a system for cognitive computing, decentralized ledger technology, and behavioral security. Claim 1: [Cognitive Integrity ID & Authentication]
A method for generating a unique authentication code by extracting non-replicable cognitive behavioral patterns—including reaction speed, typing rhythm, and causal analysis logic—to ensure absolute user integrity without relying on biological DNA.

Claim 2: [Resilient Node Survival & Emergency Shield]
A security automation technology that, upon detecting the absence of the designated administrator’s cognitive pattern or unauthorized access signals, immediately locks master authorities and transitions the system state to a decentralized mesh network to ensure eternal systemic survival.

Claim 3: [Dynamic Incentive-Driven Asset Routing]
A computerized settlement logic that automatically redirects 13% of the transaction volume as a platform fee and applies a variable 5% technical surcharge depending on the payment gateway's sovereignty status, incentivizing the use of proprietary low-cost financial networks.

Claim 4: [Algorithmic Labor & Verification Settlement]
A multi-mode cognitive verification engine that defines internal asset transfers as legitimate 'Cognitive Labor Fees' paid for the verification and optimization of the system's underlying algorithms, thereby establishing a technical basis for service-based compensation.

Claim 5: [Hierarchical Cognitive Governance & Access Control]
A system for dynamically allocating information access rights, computational priorities, and decision-making authorities based on a 16-to-256 step cognitive hierarchy (The Law of Cog), ensuring systemic stability through intellectual-meritocratic data governance.
